A Note For You ❤️

Hey Mannat,

I’ve been thinking about how to say this, and I think the simplest way is the best one. You are really, really special to me.

You make ordinary days feel lighter. A normal chat, a random laugh, even just knowing you’re around, and it somehow becomes the best part of my day. I don’t think you even realise you do that.

I keep our memories close to me. The big ones, and the tiny ones nobody else would notice. Those are the ones I end up smiling at when I’m alone.

I made this little world because I wanted to give you something that felt like you: soft, a little silly, and full of care. I hope it made you smile, even once.

And I hope we keep making moments like these. More laughs, more memories, more of us.

Thank you for being you.

Forever yours,

Rishabh ❤️

No matter how many pages this little world has, my favourite story will always be us. ❤️
